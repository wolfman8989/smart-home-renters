How we make money

Smart Home for Renters is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program that pays sites a commission for linking to Amazon.com. We also apply to manufacturer and retailer programs where they exist.

What that means for you

When you click an affiliate link and buy something, the retailer pays us a small percentage. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or go straight to the store.

How links are marked

Affiliate links are labelled in the text — “Check price on Amazon” — and carry rel="sponsored". Every article that contains one also carries a disclosure box at the top and the bottom.

Editorial independence

Rankings are decided by the Renter Readiness Score before any affiliate relationship is considered. Three concrete consequences you can check on the site:

  • We recommend the $35 camera over the $99 one when it scores higher.
  • We tell you not to buy a thermostat in a rental, even though thermostats pay well.
  • We score products we have no program with exactly the same way as ones we do.

What we do not do

  • We do not accept payment for a review, a ranking or a placement.
  • We do not publish a device we have not tested or thoroughly researched.
  • We do not hide a product’s flaws to protect a commission.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you think a ranking is wrong, email disclosure@smarthomerent.casa.

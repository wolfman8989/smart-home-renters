How No-Drill Doorbells Work

Instead of hardwiring into your doorbell circuit, battery-powered doorbells:

  1. Mount with adhesive strips or a no-drill mount (included)
  2. Connect to your Wi-Fi network
  3. Send alerts to your phone when someone rings or motion is detected
  4. Store video in the cloud (subscription) or locally (no subscription)

What you DON'T need:

  • Existing doorbell wiring
  • An electrician
  • Drilling into your doorframe
  • Landlord permission (for most)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can renters install a video doorbell without landlord permission? Yes, for battery-powered and peephole models. No drilling or wiring means no landlord permission needed. Always check your lease for specific restrictions.

Do video doorbells work in apartments with poor Wi-Fi? Wi-Fi doorbells need solid 2.4GHz signal. For thick walls, consider a Wi-Fi extender. Eufy's local storage works even with intermittent Wi-Fi.

Can I take a video doorbell when I move? Yes. Unstick adhesive mounts, unscrew peephole cams, pack and reinstall at your new place. Most take 5 minutes to reinstall.

Do I need a subscription for video doorbells? Not always. Eufy offers local storage (no subscription). Ring, Blink, Arlo, and Nest require subscriptions for video history.

What if my apartment has a peephole? Get the Ring Peephole Cam. It replaces your existing peephole with a camera. Zero installation damage, screws back in the original when you move.

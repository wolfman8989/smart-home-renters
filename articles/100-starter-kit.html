Setup Guide (15 Minutes Total)

Step 1: Set Up the Echo Dot (5 min)

  1. Plug in Echo Dot
  2. Download Alexa app on your phone
  3. Follow app instructions to connect
  4. Connect to your Wi-Fi
  5. Test: "Alexa, what's the weather?"

Step 2: Set Up Smart Plugs (4 min)

  1. Plug smart plug into outlet
  2. Download Kasa app
  3. Follow app instructions to connect
  4. Name each plug (e.g., "Living Room Lamp", "Coffee Maker")
  5. Test: "Alexa, turn on Living Room Lamp"

Step 3: Set Up Smart Bulbs (4 min)

  1. Screw bulb into existing fixture
  2. Turn on the light switch
  3. Download Kasa app (same app as plugs)
  4. Follow app instructions to connect
  5. Name each bulb (e.g., "Bedroom Light", "Desk Lamp")
  6. Test: "Alexa, turn on Bedroom Light"

Step 4: Create Routines (2 min)

  1. Open Alexa app → Routines
  2. Create "Good Morning" routine:
    • Trigger: 7:00 AM
    • Actions: Turn on Bedroom Light, Turn on Coffee Maker
  3. Create "Good Night" routine:
    • Trigger: 10:00 PM
    • Actions: Turn off all lights, Turn off all plugs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I build a smart home for under $100? Yes. The $100 starter kit (smart plug + smart bulb + Echo Dot) gives you voice control, scheduling, and remote access for $95.

Do smart plugs work with any device? Yes. Smart plugs work with any device that plugs into a standard outlet: lamps, fans, coffee makers, space heaters, etc.

Can I use smart bulbs in any fixture? Yes, as long as the bulb type matches (E26, E27, GU10, etc.). Most standard fixtures use E26 (US) or E27 (EU).

Do I need a hub? No. The Kasa smart plug and bulb connect directly to Wi-Fi. The Echo Dot acts as the voice control hub.

Can I take everything when I move? Yes. Unplug the plugs, unscrew the bulbs, pack the Echo Dot. Reinstall at your new place in 15 minutes.

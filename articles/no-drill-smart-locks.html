How No-Drill Smart Locks Work

Instead of replacing your entire lock, these devices attach to the interior side of your existing deadbolt. They grip the thumb turn (the part you twist to lock/unlock) and motorize it. Your exterior key still works normally.

What you need:

  • A standard single-cylinder deadbolt (most apartments have this)
  • A flat interior thumb turn (not a knob-style)
  • Clean door surface for adhesive (if applicable)

What you DON'T need:

  • Drilling
  • New keys
  • Landlord permission (in most cases)
  • An electrician

How to Install (Step-by-Step)

SwitchBot Lock Pro (Adhesive Method)

  1. Clean the interior door surface with alcohol wipe
  2. Position the lock over your deadbolt's thumb turn
  3. Press firmly for 30 seconds
  4. Open the SwitchBot app and calibrate
  5. Test lock/unlock 5 times
  6. Set up auto-lock (optional)

Time: 5 minutes | Tools: None | Damage: Zero

Wyze Lock (Screwdriver Method)

  1. Remove existing interior lock assembly (2 screws)
  2. Attach Wyze mounting plate
  3. Insert Wyze lock
  4. Tighten screws
  5. Open Wyze app and calibrate

Time: 10 minutes | Tools: Screwdriver | Damage: Minor (screw holes already exist)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install a smart lock in a rental apartment? Yes. No-drill smart locks attach to your existing deadbolt from the inside. They don't modify the lock or door, so landlord permission is typically not required. Always check your lease.

Will a smart lock void my lease? No. No-drill smart locks don't alter the door or lock mechanism. Your exterior key still works normally. The lock is removable with zero trace.

What if my landlord changes the lock? If your landlord replaces the lock, you'll need to recalibrate your smart lock. The adhesive mount works with any standard deadbolt thumb turn.

Can I use a smart lock with a knob (not thumb turn)? Most no-drill smart locks require a flat thumb turn. If your lock has a knob-style turn, you may need a different solution (like a keypad lock that replaces the exterior).

How long do batteries last? SwitchBot: 6-9 months. Wyze: 12 months. August: 3-6 months. Level: 12 months. All use standard batteries (CR123A or AA).

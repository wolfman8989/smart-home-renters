Frequently Asked Questions

Can renters install smart home devices without landlord permission? Yes, for most devices. No-drill, battery-powered, and plug-in devices don't require landlord permission. However, always check your lease for specific restrictions.

Do smart home devices work in apartments with poor Wi-Fi? Wi-Fi devices need solid 2.4GHz signal. For thick walls, consider a Wi-Fi extender or Thread-based devices (which use mesh networking).

Can I take smart home devices when I move? Yes — that's the beauty of renter-friendly devices. Everything in this guide is portable. Unplug, pack, and reinstall at your new place.

What's the easiest smart home device to install in a rental? Smart plugs. Plug into any outlet, connect to Wi-Fi, done in under 2 minutes.

Setup Order

  1. Plugs — five minutes, instant payoff
  2. Lock — weekend job, test the auto-unlock radius properly
  3. Hub 2 — needs a warm afternoon to learn the AC codes
  4. Camera + doorbell — do them together, they share an app if you use Ring
  5. Lighting — the fun one, save it for when you need a win
  6. Security sensors — last, because you want to test every window

What $500 Does Not Buy You

  • Outdoor cameras that survive weather. Apartment balconies are usually fine, but check your lease before mounting anything outside.
  • Whole-home audio. Skip it. Renters move and speakers are heavy.
  • Smart blinds. $89–129 each and they belong on the blinds page, not in a starter kit.
  • Anything wired. No smart switches, no in-wall dimmers, no doorbell transformers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need all of this on day one? No. Buy in the setup order above and stop whenever you like. Each step works on its own.

Will this all work together? Mostly via Matter and Alexa/Google. The Aqara sensors and the SwitchBot hub both bridge to Matter, and the Ring doorbell stays in the Ring app. Two apps is normal; four is not needed here.

What if my building has no door I control? Skip the doorbell and the lock, and put the money into a second camera and a better alarm.

Can I install the doorbell without drilling? Yes — Ring sells a no-drill mount, or use a strong adhesive bracket on a flat door frame. Do not mount it on a shared corridor wall.

How long does the whole setup take? About four hours spread over a weekend, plus a week of living with it to tune the automations.

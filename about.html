Smart Home for Renters
Written by renters, for renters. Every device is installed in an apartment where drilling is not an option.

Why this site exists

Almost every smart home guide assumes you own your home. “Just replace the thermostat.” “Wire the smart switch into the junction box.” “Mount the camera with the included screws.” None of that works when the wall is not yours.

So we only cover devices that a lease actually allows: things that stick on, clip on, or plug in — and that come off again without a mark when you move.

How we test

Our method

Every device is installed in a real apartment with no drilling allowed. We live with it for at least two weeks, then remove it and check the wall. We score install ease, removability, portability and whether a landlord would object — then we publish the score, good or bad.

Every device is scored on our Renter Readiness Score:

DimensionPointsWhat it measures
Install ease0–33 = plug-in only, 0 = drilling or wiring
Removability0–33 = zero trace, 0 = permanent damage
No-drill0–22 = zero holes, 0 = requires holes
Portability0–11 = fits in a moving box
Lease compliance0–11 = no permission needed

Maximum score: 10/10. A device that scores below 6 does not get recommended, no matter how good it is for a homeowner.

Our standards

  • Cons are as prominent as pros. If something is bad, it says so.
  • No paid reviews. We do not accept payment for coverage or placement.
  • Real testing. Nothing is recommended from a spec sheet alone.
  • Regular updates. Prices and availability change; pages carry a date.
  • Clear disclosure. Affiliate links are marked and explained.

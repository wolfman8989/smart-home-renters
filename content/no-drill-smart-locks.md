## How No-Drill Smart Locks Work

Instead of replacing your entire lock, these devices attach to the **interior side** of your existing deadbolt. They grip the thumb turn (the part you twist to lock/unlock) and motorize it. Your exterior key still works normally.

**What you need:**
- A standard single-cylinder deadbolt (most apartments have this)
- A flat interior thumb turn (not a knob-style)
- Clean door surface for adhesive (if applicable)

**What you DON'T need:**
- Drilling
- New keys
- Landlord permission (in most cases)
- An electrician

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I install a smart lock in a rental apartment?**
Yes. No-drill smart locks attach to your existing deadbolt from the inside. They don't modify the lock or door, so landlord permission is typically not required. Always check your lease.

**Will a smart lock void my lease?**
No. No-drill smart locks don't alter the door or lock mechanism. Your exterior key still works normally. The lock is removable with zero trace.

**What if my landlord changes the lock?**
If your landlord replaces the lock, you'll need to recalibrate your smart lock. The adhesive mount works with any standard deadbolt thumb turn.

**Can I use a smart lock with a knob (not thumb turn)?**
Most no-drill smart locks require a flat thumb turn. If your lock has a knob-style turn, you may need a different solution (like a keypad lock that replaces the exterior).

**How long do batteries last?**
SwitchBot: 6-9 months. Wyze: 12 months. August: 3-6 months. Level: 12 months. All use standard batteries (CR123A or AA).

**Can I build a smart home for under $50?**

**Do cheap smart home devices work with Alexa and Google Home?**
Most do. TP-Link Kasa, Wyze, Govee, and Amazon Smart Plug all work with both Alexa and Google Home. Sensibo Sky works with Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit. SwitchBot Bot needs the SwitchBot Hub for voice control.

**Are cheap smart plugs safe?**
Yes, if you buy from reputable brands (TP-Link, Wyze, Amazon, Eve). Look for UL certification. Avoid no-name brands from random sellers — they can be fire hazards. All plugs recommended here are UL-listed.

**Do I need a hub for any of these?**
No. Every device on this list connects directly to Wi-Fi. No hub, no bridge, no extra cost. The only exception is SwitchBot Bot, which needs the SwitchBot Hub for remote access and voice control (but works on a timer without it).

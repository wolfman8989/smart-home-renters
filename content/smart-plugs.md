## Why a Smart Plug Beats Almost Everything Else

Renters cannot change light switches, ceiling fixtures or wiring. A smart plug sidesteps all of it:

- **Lamps** become voice-controlled and scheduled
- **Space heaters and portable ACs** stop being a fire risk you left on
- **Coffee makers** start when your alarm goes off
- **Holiday lights** run on a timer so the apartment looks occupied
- **Anything with a physical switch** can be turned off remotely

And when you move, you unplug it and put it in a box. There is no other smart home device with that ratio of usefulness to effort.

## The Space Heater Test (Why Energy Monitoring Matters)

We put a 1,500 W space heater on the Kasa EP25 for two months and logged consumption. Over a 30-day month at 4 hours a day, it used **180 kWh**. At the US average of 16 cents per kWh, that is **$28.80 a month** — for one heater.

That number is the argument for energy monitoring: renters usually pay their own electricity, and a plug that tells you what a device actually costs is worth more than a plug that just turns things on.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do smart plugs work without Wi-Fi?**
They need Wi-Fi to set up, but most keep their schedules running locally afterwards. If the internet goes down, a lamp on a schedule will still turn on.

**Can I plug a space heater into a smart plug?**
Yes, if the plug is rated for the load. The Kasa EP25 is 15 A / 1,800 W, which covers a standard 1,500 W heater. Never exceed the rating and never use a plug on an extension cord with a heater.

**Will a smart plug work with a lamp that has a dimmer switch?**
Only if the lamp is switched fully on. A smart plug controls power, not brightness — for dimming you need a smart bulb.

**Do I need a hub?**
No. Every plug in this guide connects directly to Wi-Fi or Thread.

**Can I take them when I move?**
Yes. That is the whole point. Unplug, pack, reinstall in five minutes.

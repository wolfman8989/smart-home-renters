## What $500 Does Not Buy You

- **Outdoor cameras that survive weather.** Apartment balconies are usually fine, but check your lease before mounting anything outside.
- **Whole-home audio.** Skip it. Renters move and speakers are heavy.
- **Smart blinds.** $89–129 each and they belong on the [blinds page](/articles/no-drill-blinds.html), not in a starter kit.
- **Anything wired.** No smart switches, no in-wall dimmers, no doorbell transformers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need all of this on day one?**
No. Buy in the setup order above and stop whenever you like. Each step works on its own.

**Will this all work together?**
Mostly via Matter and Alexa/Google. The Aqara sensors and the SwitchBot hub both bridge to Matter, and the Ring doorbell stays in the Ring app. Two apps is normal; four is not needed here.

**What if my building has no door I control?**
Skip the doorbell and the lock, and put the money into a second camera and a better alarm.

**Can I install the doorbell without drilling?**
Yes — Ring sells a no-drill mount, or use a strong adhesive bracket on a flat door frame. Do not mount it on a shared corridor wall.

**How long does the whole setup take?**
About four hours spread over a weekend, plus a week of living with it to tune the automations.

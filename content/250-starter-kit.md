## What This Kit Does Not Include (On Purpose)

- **A security system.** Real monitored systems start around $200 and deserve their own budget. See [renter security systems](/articles/renter-security-systems.html).
- **A video doorbell.** Apartments often have shared entryways where a doorbell is not yours to mount. If yours is a private door, add one later.
- **Smart bulbs.** Plugs cover lamps more cheaply. Bulbs matter when you want dimming and colour — see [smart lighting for renters](/articles/smart-lighting-renters.html).
- **A hub for everything.** Matter means you increasingly do not need one.

## Setup Order (Do It In This Order)

1. **Plugs first, day one.** They take five minutes and change your daily routine immediately.
2. **Lock second.** Do it on a weekend when you have time to test the auto-unlock radius.
3. **AC controller third.** You need a warm afternoon to confirm it learned the right codes.
4. **Camera fourth.** Set it up last so it is not watching you install everything.
5. **LED strip whenever.** It is the fun one; save it for a day you need a win.

## When You Move

Every item in this kit comes with you:

- Unplug the four smart plugs
- Take the Wyze Lock off the inside of the door and refit the original thumb turn
- Unplug the Hub 2
- Peel the LED strip (gently, with a little warmth)
- Unplug the camera

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is $250 enough for a whole apartment?**
For a one-bedroom, yes. For a two-bedroom you will want a second camera and maybe two more plugs — budget $320.

**Do I need a hub?**
No. Every device in this kit connects directly, and the Hub 2 is for the AC, not for the others.

**What if I have no smart home experience?**
Start with the plugs alone. If you like it, come back for the rest. The kit is ordered so that stopping after step one still leaves you with something useful.

**Will any of this annoy my landlord?**
No drilling, no wiring, no permanent fixtures. The lock changes the inside of the door only, and it goes back before you move out.

**Should I wait for a sale?**
Amazon discounts this exact stack heavily around Prime Day and Black Friday. The plugs and the LED strip routinely hit 40% off.

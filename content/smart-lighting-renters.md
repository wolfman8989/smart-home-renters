## How Renter-Friendly Smart Lighting Works

Smart lighting for renters falls into three categories:

1. **Smart bulbs** — Replace your existing bulbs. Screw in, connect to Wi-Fi or hub. No wiring.
2. **Smart switches (peel-and-stick)** — Adhesive-mounted wireless switches that control smart bulbs. No wiring.
3. **Smart plugs + lamps** — Plug a smart plug into an outlet, plug a lamp into the smart plug. Done.

**What you DON'T need:**
- An electrician
- Wiring changes
- Landlord permission (for most)
- New fixtures

## How to Set Up Smart Lighting in a Rental

### Step 1: Start with Bulbs
Replace your most-used bulbs with smart bulbs. Start with the living room and bedroom. Don't replace every bulb at once — start with 2-3 and expand.

### Step 2: Add a Switch
If you have Hue bulbs, add a Dimmer Switch. Mount it with adhesive next to your existing light switch. Now you have physical control without touching wiring.

### Step 3: Add Accent Lighting
LED strips behind TV, under cabinets, or along baseboards. Use adhesive mounting. Remove with heat (hair dryer) when moving.

### Step 4: Create Scenes
Set up "Movie Night" (dim lights, warm color), "Morning" (bright, cool white), "Away" (lights on timer). Automate with Alexa/Google routines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I install smart lighting in a rental without an electrician?**
Yes. Smart bulbs, smart plugs, and peel-and-stick switches require no electrician. Just screw in bulbs or plug in devices.

**Will smart bulbs work with my existing fixtures?**
Yes. Smart bulbs (A19, BR30, etc.) fit standard fixtures. Just match the bulb type (E26, E27, GU10, etc.) to your fixture.

**Can I take smart lighting when I move?**
Yes. Everything in this guide is portable. Unscrew bulbs, unplug strips, peel off switches. Reinstall at your new place.

**Do smart lights use more electricity?**
No. LED smart bulbs use 80% less energy than incandescent. Standby power draw is minimal (~0.5W per bulb).

**What if my landlord says no?**
Most landlords don't object to smart bulbs (they're just bulbs). For adhesive switches, check your lease — some prohibit adhesive on walls. If unsure, mount on furniture or use magnetic mounts.

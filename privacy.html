Privacy Policy

\n

Last updated: October 31, 2026

\n

The short version

This is a static website. There is no account, no login, and no newsletter. We do not sell personal information, and we do not build profiles of individual readers.

What is collected

Server and CDN logs

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) to deliver pages and block abuse. Those logs are retained by Cloudflare under their own policy.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled, they are configured to record aggregate page views only — no cross-site tracking and no advertising identifiers. Analytics is used to decide which articles are worth updating.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write. That is kept only long enough to answer you.

Cookies

We do not set cookies for advertising. Third parties you reach by clicking an outbound link — Amazon, for example — may set their own cookies on their own domain. That is outside our control and covered by their policies.

Affiliate links

Outbound product links carry an affiliate tag. When you click one, the retailer is told that the visit came from this site. They are not told who you are by us, because we do not know. See the affiliate disclosure for the full list of programs.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare — hosting, CDN and DNS
  • Amazon Associates — affiliate tracking on Amazon links
  • Manufacturer CDNs — product photographs are loaded from the brand’s own image servers

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you (almost always: an email you sent), ask for it to be deleted, or ask us to stop replying. Email privacy@smarthomerent.casa.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
